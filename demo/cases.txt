Case 1, rheumatoid arthritis
----------------------------
65-year-old man, currently a carer, previously an administrator. Three-month history of stiffness in the index and middle fingers of both hands, with aching elbows, shoulders and knees. Early morning stiffness of about 45 minutes. Past medical history of hypertension and prediabetes. On examination: tender joint count 4, swollen joint count 6, involving the MCPs, PIPs and left wrist; shoulders, elbows, ankles and feet unremarkable; hips with good range of movement; knees with crepitus but not swollen. ESR 34, CRP 2.7, anti-CCP positive, rheumatoid factor positive. Impression: seropositive rheumatoid arthritis. Plan: refer urgently to rheumatology; started hydroxychloroquine at first appointment and methotrexate 15 mg weekly at the second.

Case 2, polymyalgia rheumatica (NICE has no guideline)
------------------------------------------------------
71-year-old retired lagging engineer, now doing building work for friends. Polymyalgia rheumatica diagnosed by the GP seven months ago: pelvic girdle and hip pain, CRP 133, ESR 41, unable to get out of bed, no joint swelling, immediate response to prednisolone 20 mg. Referred at the time for weight loss; no sinister cause found. Steroids slowly tapering, now 5 mg, no recurrence of symptoms. DEXA shows osteopenia; the scan advice was to consider bisphosphonates, but he has loose teeth awaiting removal and a plate. Past history of MI with stent; no fractures. On examination GALS normal, Heberden's and Bouchard's nodes, pain on thoracic rotation, tender T10 to T12. Plan: no bisphosphonate while on 5 mg prednisolone; MRI spine for thoracic tenderness to exclude fracture; review on 2 mg with MRI result.

Case 3, gout
------------
Man in his late 30s, formerly a podiatrist, mainly sedentary; used to run, ski and play tennis and hockey. Recurrent joint effusions over two years affecting ankles, knees and wrist, sometimes without injury, settling over about six weeks; the last, in the left ankle, took six weeks. Some erythema during episodes, no fever. Known gout, first MTP joint, not on allopurinol; a first-ray attack was red and hot and settled within 48 hours of colchicine. Left elbow bursa on examination, otherwise no tender or swollen joints. Urate 541, normal kidney and liver function, rheumatoid factor and anti-CCP negative, white cell count and CRP normal. Low vitamin D on an over-the-counter supplement. Plan: allopurinol 100 mg started three weeks ago; leaflet on allopurinol and febuxostat; discharge.

Case 4, back pain, axial spondyloarthritis query
------------------------------------------------
62-year-old man working security, standing nine-hour shifts. Referred with aches and pains in the body, severe for the past few months, unable to attend work, with a raised rheumatoid factor. History: pain in the left buttock radiating down the left leg, sometimes shock-like to the toes; in 2022 similar pain travelling down the right leg; back pain some days but not daily. Past history of disseminated strongyloidiasis with lung infection a year ago, asthma, and a lower femur fracture in 2022. Smokes 2 to 3 a day, drinks three pints three days a week. On examination GALS normal, tone, power, reflexes and sensation intact in both legs, plantars downgoing. Impression: likely neurogenic pain. MRI lumbar spine: multilevel lower lumbar degenerative disc disease. Seen five months later with all symptoms resolved and discharged.
